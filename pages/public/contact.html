Frequently Answered Inquiries

Common questions regarding setup, access, and security

How does the multi-portal authentication (SSO) operate?
The platform utilizes a unified cryptographic session bridge. Once you register or sign in as an Administrator, the system automatically authorizes your session across the HR Portal, Teacher Portal, Finance Portal, and Public Site without requiring repetitive logins.
Can I customize the organization name and brand logo?
Yes. During organization registration on the homepage, you can input your unique organization name and upload a PNG, JPG, or SVG company emblem. The system dynamically updates the workspace titles, headers, and payslip headings to reflect your organization.
Does the platform support offline or client-only mode?
Yes. The front-end includes local storage persistence engines allowing complete standalone functionality. When connected to a MySQL/PHP backend, it synchronizes employee and student records automatically with your central database.
How are employee payslips and deductions computed?
The payroll engine handles basic salary computations, statutory tax withholdings (PAYE), pension contributions, and net pay disbursement calculations, generating print-ready digital statements for each staff member.
